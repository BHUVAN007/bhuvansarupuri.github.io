Building and Scaling UX Research at TOPdesk

Establishing the people, processes and infrastructure for continuous research.

I joined TOPdesk as its first Senior UX Researcher and helped establish the foundations of its UX research practice: the standards research is held to, ongoing access to customers, a continuous way of hearing from them, and the operations that let researchers, product managers and designers all use it.

My focus has been on making customer research repeatable, accessible and useful for product decisions, beyond any single project.

The operating model

Five parts that make research repeatable.

Each part covers a different condition for research to happen, and to be used: a shared standard, people to talk to, a regular signal, the tools and people to run it, and an organisation that knows what to ask of it.

01 · Establishing research standards

One playbook, so a study means the same thing in every team.

I own the research playbook. Alongside it I developed reusable methods and templates and the guidance that goes with them, so studies are planned, run and reported consistently, and the quality of research does not depend on who happens to run it.

  • Research playbook. The shared reference for how research is done at TOPdesk, which I own and maintain.
  • Reusable methods and templates. Starting points for common study types, so teams are not designing each study from scratch.
  • Guidance and quality. Making research practice more consistent and accessible across product teams.

The Research Copilot builds on this: it is grounded in TOPdesk's research standards and its democratisation matrix, which sets out which research product roles can run on their own and which stays with a trained researcher.

03 · Introducing continuous discovery

A regular Voice of Customer signal in my product domain.

I introduced a continuous Voice of Customer programme within my product domain. It gives a consistent way to understand customer needs and to bring evidence into product discussions as a matter of routine, rather than only when a project asks for it.

04 · Scaling research operations

Operations for researchers, product managers and designers.

As UX Research Lead, my responsibilities expanded from running research to building what research runs on. The goal is research operations that support not only researchers, but the product managers and designers who also need customer evidence.

The research infrastructure I built for this includes Research Buddy, which puts existing evidence behind one question, and the Research Copilot, which guides product roles through research they can run themselves. TOPdesk Atlas connects that evidence to the product itself.

05 · Building research adoption

Helping the organisation understand what research can do.

I facilitate workshops and present research findings and methods to product leadership and the wider Product & Technology organisation, through workshops, presentations, coaching and knowledge sharing. The aim is a shared understanding of research processes, customer needs and opportunities.
