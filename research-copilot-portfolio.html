Why I built it

TOPdesk Research Copilot

If you are a PM, a designer, or a support lead at TOPdesk, doing research usually means waiting for a researcher, or skipping it. I wanted to open that up. This is a copilot that lets any product role run real research on their own, so a good decision does not have to wait for a specialist.

You tell it what you are trying to figure out. It takes you to the right task and does the heavy lifting, in TOPdesk's own language.

Scope

TOPdesk has a democratisation matrix that sets out which research any product role can run, and which stays with a trained researcher. The line is drawn on methodology, depth and rigour. This copilot works inside that matrix rather than around it: it opens up the tasks the matrix marks as safe to democratise, and leaves the studies that call for a specialist where they belong.

What it does

Say what you are doing. It runs the task.

Talk to it or type. It works out which research task you need, opens it, and grounds everything in TOPdesk's real product docs, so it does not sound like generic UX advice.

  • Talk or type what you are working on. It routes you to the right task and brings your context along.
  • 26 tasks across the whole research lifecycle, from framing a study to proving its impact.
Inside a task

Framing a study, without knowing the theory.

Take the most common starting point. You describe the problem in plain words, and the task pressure-tests it: it flags biased or fuzzy questions, names the assumption worth testing first, and suggests a method. You end up with a real plan, not a blank page.

Under the hood

Agents, skills, and workflows.

The whole system is config, not code. Every task is a skill, skills belong to a method agent, and skills chain into workflows. That is what makes it easy to grow and keeps every task consistent.

Every skill belongs to one of three method agents. The stakeholder, a PM or a lead, is a role in the work, not an agent, so the tool never pretends a person is automated.

- name: affinity-synthesis owner-agent: qual purpose: cluster notes into themes with evidence links inputs: raw notes, quotes, transcript excerpts outputs: theme clusters, supporting quotes, evidence ratings guardrails: do not present qualitative themes as population stats
One skill is one spec. A single engine reads it, asks Claude for structured output, grounds it in the TOPdesk docs, and holds the guardrails. Adding a task is a few lines of YAML, not a new screen.

Seven workflows chain the skills

A workflow is an ordered run of skills with review gates, so bigger jobs stay on rails.

How a request flows

Three ways in

Voice or text. A router picks the task.
The task hub
Pick a task from the home screen.
A direct link
/task?skill=… opens one task.
↓
Node server
A small plain HTTP server routes each request. No framework.
↓
The skill-runner
Reads the skill's spec, asks Claude for structured JSON, grounds it in the TOPdesk docs, and enforces the guardrails. If the AI is off, a plain rule-based fallback runs instead.
↓
Claude
Structured JSON output.
TOPdesk docs
