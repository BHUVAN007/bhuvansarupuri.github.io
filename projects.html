TOPdesk Research Copilot

A copilot that lets any product role run real research on their own, so a decision does not have to wait for a specialist. Say or type what you are working on, and it routes you to the right task, grounded in TOPdesk's own product documentation. Twenty tasks across the research lifecycle, built as configuration rather than code.
