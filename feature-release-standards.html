UX RESEARCH CASE STUDY

Standardising Feature Releases

Mapping how a feature release moves across product management, UX, user guidance and marketing, through stakeholder interviews and a discovery workshop

Methods
Interviews, Workshops, Affinity Mapping
Output
Recommendations for 4 departments

Note: Collaborative research and design work completed with TOPdesk team members.

OVERVIEW

The Challenge

TOPdesk's feature releases lacked standardisation, creating inconsistent user experiences, burdened UX teams, and development efforts that failed to generate expected adoption.

Our product's feature releases lack a standardised approach

This operational inefficiency was actively undermining product value, team productivity, and customer satisfaction.

THE STAKES

Why This Matters

🎯 User Adoption at Risk

Without consistent communication and onboarding, users struggle to discover and adopt new features, reducing ROI on development investments.

⚙️ Team Efficiency Compromised

UX designers and researchers spend excessive time reinventing release processes instead of focusing on innovation.

🚀 Development Velocity Slowed

Product teams face significant bottlenecks from lack of standardised workflows, delaying time-to-market.

👥 Customer Insights Missing

Without standardised approaches, teams lack mechanisms to address perception issues, measure communication effectiveness, track feature adoption, or collect post-release feedback systematically.

METHODOLOGY

Research Approach

1

Discovery Workshop

Collaborative session with UX researchers across product teams to map current practices, decision factors, and identify gaps in existing processes.

2

Stakeholder Interviews

In-depth conversations with 7 key stakeholders: 4 Product Owners/Managers, 2 User Guidance team members, and 1 Copywriter.

3

Affinity Mapping

Systematic synthesis of research data, clustering insights into actionable categories and identifying patterns across teams.

Research Questions

  • What are the current processes and steps when introducing new features?
  • What challenges do different teams face during feature introductions?
  • How do stakeholders currently communicate and collaborate?
  • What are the main pain points or bottlenecks delaying releases?

INSIGHTS

Key Findings

Through affinity mapping, insights emerged across four critical categories:

Categorised Findings: The same material clustered into four groups: Solutions, Communication, Small tags, and Workshop findings

1. Communication Fragmentation

  • Way of Working: Product Owners drive strategy but collaboration happens inconsistently between teams and departments
  • Channel Confusion: External communication channels (What is New, emails, blog posts) differ drastically from internal ones
  • Information Overload: When new channels are added, users stop checking them and fall out of the loop

2. Process Standardisation Gaps

  • No Shared Approach: Development teams build everything from scratch with no standardised templates or guidelines
  • Time Drain: UX teams spend excessive time creating solutions for each release instead of reusing proven patterns
  • Inconsistent Quality: Without standards, release quality varies significantly across teams

3. Documentation Challenges

  • Technical Overload: Documentation portal often too detailed and technical for average users
  • Timing Issues: Portal can only be updated after release, missing critical onboarding window
  • Balance Struggle: Teams cannot find the right balance between comprehensive detail and accessibility

4. Measurement & Feedback

  • No Standard Metrics: Lack of standardised methods for measuring user satisfaction post-release
  • PO Dependency: Release decisions heavily dependent on individual Product Owner preferences
  • Scattered Cooperation: Technical writers collaborate with dev teams, but processes vary

OPPORTUNITY FRAMING

How Might We...

Transforming insights into actionable opportunities across stakeholder groups

User Guidance Teams

  • Content Timing: Get content out on time
  • Visibility: Know features releasing soon
  • Capacity: Maximize small team capacity
  • Documentation: Update portal on time
  • Collaboration: Connect dev-teams with UG
  • Info Gathering: Collect all necessary input

Development Teams

  • Standardisation: Shared efficient approach
  • Consistency: Efficient release process
  • Speed: Deliver features quickly
  • User Delight: Design enjoyable solutions
  • Engagement: Maintain feedback flow
  • Measurement: Measure user satisfaction

End Users

  • Usability Testing: Involve users effectively
  • Feature Flags: Standardise implementation
  • Feedback Loops: Create standard loops
  • Transparency: Set realistic expectations

  • Alignment: Standardise feedback process

Customers

  • Perception: Address update concerns

  • Feedback: Standard post-release loops
